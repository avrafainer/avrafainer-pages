Privacy

Hermes Workspace is a single-user personal tool. This page describes exactly what it accesses and what happens to that data. Last updated 18 November 2026.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, through Google APIs:

  • Gmail — read and send, so mail can be searched and replies drafted for approval
  • Google Drive and Docs — read, so files can be found and summarised
  • Google Calendar — read, so commitments and dates can be tracked
  • Google Contacts — read, so people can be recognised by name
  • YouTube — manage the owner's own playlists

Where the data goes

Data is stored on the owner's own computer and nowhere else. There is no server operated by this application, no analytics service and no advertising. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with any third party for any purpose.

To answer questions, the owner's own text may be sent to the language-model provider that powers her assistant, under her own account and her own billing. That is the only transfer, and it is the mechanism by which the assistant works at all.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept locally so that the assistant can answer questions about the past. The owner can delete any of it at any time, and can revoke access completely from her Google account permissions page, which stops all access immediately.
