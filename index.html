Hermes Workspace

A private, single-user assistant.

Hermes Workspace is a personal automation tool. It is used by one person, on her own accounts, to read her own mail, calendar, files and notes so that she can ask questions about her own records and have routine work done for her.

It is not a product, it has no other users, and it does not offer sign-in to anyone else.
